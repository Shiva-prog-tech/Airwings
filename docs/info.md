## About Us

Airwing Technologies is a small-scale engineering industry registered under MSME. We manufacture and machine aerospace and special-purpose components for the engineering industry.

Airwing Technologies was formed in 2022. It is managed by an experienced team highly skilled in the CNC field, with dedicated tool room personnel who specialise in designing and manufacturing aerospace components.

We have a group of passionate CAM programmers, strict quality control management, a state-of-the-art CAD-CAM system and high-precision CNC milling machines to take projects from concept to completion with total efficiency.

We have spare capacity to accommodate more jobs, and a full-fledged quality control room with all necessary measuring instruments. The instruments are calibrated periodically, as per standard practice, at approved calibration labs. We have qualified manpower in the design, quality and production areas, and we work around the clock.

### Commitment & Vision
We embrace the challenge of finding timely, accurate solutions to intricate design and tooling problems by constantly investing in new technology, tools and equipment, and upgrading the skills of our employees. We are a people-centric organisation set out to achieve collective success for our customers, employees and management by ethical means and with high integrity.

### Mission
To provide zero-defect precision machined components for aerospace, avionics and airborne systems while ensuring zero wastage, and to achieve an INR 10 Crore turnover by 2030.

### Focus
- Design and development of new products for the aerospace industry
- Manufacture and supply of sub-assemblies and high-precision components to Indian and overseas customers
- Development of high-quality, low-cost export items for engineering applications
- Measurement services using a portable height gauge

### Quality Policy
We at Airwing Technologies are committed to total customer satisfaction in terms of quality, delivery and service through state-of-the-art engineering techniques. We also strive for continual improvement in our quality management system, technology, processes and competency.

### Certification Statement
Our company is committed to ultimate customer satisfaction by manufacturing and supplying precision machined components to the highest quality standards with timely delivery.

### Facility
We have 10,000 sq ft of working area with good store and fitting facilities, a CAD-CAM room, a quality room and an office. We handle customer raw material and components with care, identifying each by batch and material grade and storing them separately.

We specialise in manufacturing machined parts that require a series of different operations. We analyse the design, then manufacture, assemble and deliver the end product ready for its application.

We have a separate design team to design new products such as components, jigs and fixtures. We also provide drafting for parts.

We make assembly parts for machine manufacturers, SPM (special purpose machine) manufacturers and fabricated-item manufacturers. We also provide CMM inspection when required.
